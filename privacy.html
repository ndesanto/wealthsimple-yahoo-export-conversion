Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2026

Overview

We are committed to protecting your privacy. This converter runs entirely in your browser and does not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal information or financial data.

Data Collection

No Data Collection: This tool does not collect any personal information. Your CSV files are processed locally in your browser and are never sent to our servers or any third parties. There are no accounts to create, no data collection, and no tracking. Once you close your browser, nothing is stored.

How the Tool Works

  • Your CSV file is read and processed entirely within your web browser
  • No data is transmitted to any external server
  • No copies of your data are stored on our servers
  • Once you close the browser, nothing persists except what you choose to save locally

Third-Party Services

This site is hosted on a web server and may use standard web analytics that do not identify you personally. Google AdSense may display ads and collect anonymous data about site usage in accordance with their privacy policies.

Security

Since processing happens in your browser, your data security depends on your own computer's security. We recommend:

  • Using a secure, up-to-date browser
  • Not sharing your CSV files over unsecured networks
  • Verifying converted data before using it for financial purposes

Cookies and Tracking

We do not use cookies to track your behavior or identify you. The site may use essential cookies only for basic functionality (these are not tracking cookies). You can disable cookies in your browser settings without affecting the tool's functionality.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy to reflect changes in our practices. Any significant changes will be noted herein. Your continued use of this tool constitutes acceptance of any updates.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy or how we handle your data, please reach out through the GitHub repository.

Your Rights

Since we don't collect any data, there's nothing to request, delete, or modify. Your information is entirely within your control from the moment you use this tool.
